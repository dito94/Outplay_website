Choosing and planning

What is the difference between Balance and Infinity?

Both are bioclimatic pergolas with aluminium louvres that rotate from 0° to 120°. Balance uses one motor and the louvres rotate only. Infinity uses two motors, so the louvres both rotate and retract fully to one end, opening the roof completely to the sky.

Which roof should I choose: louvres, PVC fabric, glass or insulated panels?

Louvres give the finest control over light and airflow. Three-layer Blackout PVC (Flat and Curved series) is 100% waterproof, blocks up to 89% of UV and withstands gusts of up to 117 km/h. A glass roof, retractable or fixed, lets in the most daylight. Insulated panels insulate best and also work well as carports. We will help you choose during the consultation.

Can a pergola be enclosed as a winter garden?

Yes. With vertical glazing (sliding panels, a guillotine system or folding glass) a pergola becomes a winter garden you can use in the colder months too. Sliding systems and the guillotine are fully compatible with the entire bioclimatic pergola range. For PVC roofs, the version with the fabric built into the guide rails is the best basis for a winter garden.

Can the pergola be attached to the building?

Yes. Pergolas can be freestanding or fixed to the façade, with two front posts, with no posts between two walls, or, for the Flat and Curved series, hung from the façade on tension rods. For large areas, several modules are coupled into one structure.

What colours are available?

Aluminium structures are powder-coated in any RAL colour. The retractable glass roof is also available in matt, gloss, textured and wood-effect finishes, and dormakaba doors can be anodised. Fabrics for ZIP screens and awnings come in a wide range of colours and textures.

What extras can a pergola have?

LED lighting (dimmable RGB on louvred pergolas), heaters, a sound system, ZIP sun screens and vertical glazing can all be integrated into the structure. On the insulated-panel roof, LED lighting, sound or radiant heating fit neatly into the ceiling.

Quotes and site visits

How does a quote request with approximate sizes work?

Choose a product, enter approximate dimensions and the options you want, and send the request. We reply with an indicative quote. If you decide to go ahead, we arrange a site visit and measurement, and then send a final quote with the exact specification.

Why are there no prices on the site?

Every system is designed and made to measure. The price depends on size, configuration, roof and glass type, automation and extras. That is why we prepare an individual quote: first an indicative one based on your dimensions, then a final one after the site visit.

Do I need exact measurements?

No. Approximate sizes, such as the width and depth of your terrace, are enough for an indicative quote. One of our specialists takes exact measurements during the site visit. Photos of the space help too.

Do you work with restaurants, hotels and commercial premises?

Yes. Alongside private homes, we work on restaurants, hotels, offices and public buildings, from glazing roof terraces and restaurant gardens to dormakaba automatic entrance doors. We offer a complete service: consultation, design, manufacturing, installation and set-up.

Installation and permits

How long do manufacturing and installation take?

It depends on the system, the size and the scope of the project, and the timing is stated in the quote. Structures are made to measure, and installation and set-up of the automation are carried out by our own team.

Do I need a foundation?

It depends on the structure and on what is there now: a concrete slab, paving, a terrace or a roof. How the system is anchored, and whether a foundation is needed, is decided during the site visit and design.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the type and size of the structure and on the property. For some installations, especially those attached to a building or in a block of flats, approval from the municipality or consent from the other owners may be needed. We will advise you during the site visit, and it is worth confirming the requirements with your local municipality.

Technology and everyday use

What happens to the louvres when it rains?

With the louvres closed, the roof keeps out rain and snow. Water collects in the built-in gutters around the perimeter and runs down through the posts, which double as concealed downpipes. Each louvre also has added thermal and acoustic insulation.

How much wind and snow can the structures take?

The Flat and Curved PVC-roof pergolas withstand gusts of up to 117 km/h. Our bioclimatic systems are designed to stand up to wind, rain and snow, and the fixed glass and insulated-panel roofs to heavy snowfall and strong winds. Actual loads depend on the size and location and are taken into account at the design stage.

Can the systems work automatically?

Automated pergolas are operated by remote control. Sun protection systems can be fitted with wind, sun and rain sensors that retract the fabric in strong wind or extend it in strong sun, and can be connected to a SMART home system. Shell awnings can also be controlled from a mobile device.

Perforated or blackout ZIP fabric: what is the difference?

Micro-perforated fabric diffuses sunlight, lets air through and keeps the view out while blocking the view in. Blackout fabrics are opaque and give complete darkness, for rooms where you need it.

How does the windbreak work without electricity?

Telescopic windbreaks have integrated hydraulic dampers. A light press of the button raises or lowers the upper glass panel smoothly and effortlessly, with no electrical supply.

What is Magic Switch touchless opening?

Magic Switch is a radar sensor for dormakaba ES 200 automatic doors: the door opens with a light wave of the hand in front of the sensor, with no contact. A discreet foot button can be added too. It suits medical facilities, laboratories, food businesses and public buildings.

Care and warranty

How much maintenance do the systems need?

The structures are made of high-grade powder-coated aluminium that resists corrosion and weather, and they need no complicated maintenance. Fixed roofs have no moving parts at all. You will receive care recommendations for your specific system at installation.

How do you clean guillotine glazing?

The system has a patented mechanism that lets the innermost and middle panels tilt inwards. You can safely reach the outer face of the glass and clean it yourself, without rope-access specialists or outside help.

What warranty do you offer?

Warranty terms depend on the system and its components and are set out in the quote and contract. We work with profile systems and components from established manufacturers.

Can I add glazing or ZIP screens later?

In many cases, yes. The pergolas are designed to take extra elements such as lighting, sun protection and glazing, and sun protection systems can also be fitted to existing structures. It is best to plan them at the design stage so they can be fully integrated.
