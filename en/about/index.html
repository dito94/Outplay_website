About OUTPLAY

For twenty years we have planned, designed, manufactured and installed bespoke architectural solutions.

Sofia 20 years of experience

The company

OUTPLAY has 20 years of experience in planning, designing, manufacturing and installing bespoke and non-standard architectural solutions. Based in Sofia, the company has a modern production facility and advanced equipment that turn each concept into something real: good-looking, practical and built to last.

Behind our work is a team of conscientious specialists with long experience and strong qualifications. Our first priority is a finished product of uncompromising quality that meets strict European standards.

We partner with leading international makers of profile systems and technology, including REYNAERS, ALUMIL, ETEM, SCHÜCO, HILTI, WÜRTH, SIKA, DORMA, SOMFY, HÖRMANN, FUNDERMAX and ALUCOBOND. We keep up with new technology and bring in modern materials and smart systems to give our clients contemporary design, function and peace of mind.

20 years of experience in planning, designing, manufacturing and installing architectural solutions

What we do

Research, design and construction of bespoke building solutions and structures for residential and public buildings

Explore the products

  • Bioclimatic systems and smart sun protection

    Automated roof structures and bioclimatic winter gardens: aluminium and glass roofs, pergolas with three-layer PVC roofs, sliding systems, guillotine glazing, telescopic glass windbreaks and sun screens.

  • Windows, doors and glazing

    PVC and aluminium systems, thermally broken or not, for doors, windows and façades, with a wide choice of glazing units and colours. We pay special attention to sliding systems: folding, lift-and-slide (HST), parallel sliding (VW) and more.

  • Interior glass and office solutions

    Glass doors, display cases and shower enclosures, acoustic partition walls, and sliding and fixed glass systems for flexible workplaces.

  • Façades and exterior glazing

    Glass curtain walls and ventilated façades in aluminium, HPL, zinc and ceramic on steel structures, plus aluminium and glass balustrades.

Experience and aesthetics

We believe every space has potential that the right mix of technology and design can bring out.

We build long-term partnerships based on trust and quality, combining the know-how of leading manufacturers with our own expertise to turn your idea into lasting architecture. From the first concept to the final installation, we stay focused on reliability and aesthetics.

How we work

A complete service, from expert advice and precise design to final installation and set-up.

  1. 01

    Request with approximate sizes

    Choose a product and send us rough dimensions and the options you want, through the form, by phone or on Viber.

  2. 02

    Indicative quote

    Based on your dimensions, we send an indicative quote and recommend the right system.

  3. 03

    Site visit and measuring

    One of our specialists visits the site, takes exact measurements and goes through the installation details with you.

  4. 04

    Design and visualisation

    We design the system for your site and present the solution together with the final quote.

  5. 05

    Manufacturing

    The structure is made to measure, in your chosen RAL colour and with the options you selected.

  6. 06

    Installation and set-up

    Our team installs the system, sets up the automation and shows you how to use it.

Timing and the final price are confirmed in the quote after the site visit.
