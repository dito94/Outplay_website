Harmonia

Harmonia series folding glass system

Modular freedom, flexible zoning.

Centrally hung glass panels that rotate through 360° and move independently along the track, working as a movable wall or a series of pivot doors.

Overview

Harmonia moves in a different way: the carrier is mounted at the centre of each glass panel. Hung at its midpoint, every leaf can rotate freely through 360° and travel along the track in perfect balance.

Central suspension spreads the weight of the glass evenly for smooth, effortless sliding. It is ideal for flexible partition walls in offices, restaurants and retail spaces.

Movable wall and pivot doors

Harmonia works both as a movable wall and as a series of individual pivot doors. The panels move independently and need no floor guides, so they can be grouped in different parts of the room to clear the way completely.

The technology allows very tall, wide glass walls that fold away compactly when open. Dedicated mechanisms lock each panel when closed, and the precise fit and seals keep out dust and noise.

Complete transformation, precise engineering

Folding systems free up the opening almost entirely, so the edge of the room practically disappears. High-quality roller systems keep operation light and silent even with large, heavy panels.

Glass is tempered or laminated, tinted options come in several shades, and the aluminium profiles can be made in any RAL colour.

Features

  • Centrally hung panels
  • Free 360° rotation
  • Each panel moves independently
  • Movable wall and individual pivot doors
  • No floor guides
  • Panels can be grouped in different zones
  • Each panel locks when closed
  • Seals against dust and noise
