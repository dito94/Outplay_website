Overview

Curved brings a sculptural, generous presence to any outdoor space with its arched roof. The flowing silhouette gives a project real architectural character, and the curve of the vault helps rainwater run off.

It withstands gusts of up to 117 km/h and filters out up to 89% of harmful UV radiation. With a 100% waterproof canopy, the vaulted roof keeps you comfortable whatever the weather does.

The arch suits both classic and contemporary exteriors, and integrated LED lighting picks out its curves at night. It is fully compatible with windbreaks and glazing.

Traditional in spirit, modern in engineering

At the heart of the aluminium frame is a three-layer PVC membrane with integrated Blackout technology, fully waterproof and fire-retardant. It stops the sun and shields against the weather, pairing the classic look of an awning with modern automation.

The choice is between the flowing curves of Curved and the crisp elegance of Flat. Both series can be freestanding or fixed to the façade, with profiles in a wide range of RAL colours.

Hanging or built-in fabric

Fabric hanging beneath the guide rails creates a light, Mediterranean effect and cool summer shade. Although designed mainly for sun protection, it can be combined with glazing against wind and rain.

Fabric built into the guide rails gives a monolithic look and better insulation. The tight fit of the components handles rainwater cleanly and holds in heat, making it the ideal basis for a winter garden with vertical glazing.

Configurations

  • Façade-mounted with tension rods

    The structure is fixed to the façade with tension rods. A good fit for public and private buildings where support posts are not possible.

  • Façade-mounted with two posts

    The structure is fixed to the façade and supported by two posts at the front. Suits commercial premises as well as homes with gardens or terraces.

  • Freestanding and fully self-supporting

    Either two motors with two independent roof sections that gather in the centre, or a single motor on just four posts with the roof retracting to one end.

  • Central supporting beam

    A freestanding structure with a supporting beam and two motors for two independent retractable roofs that gather in the centre. No extra posts, for residential and public spaces with limited room for supports.
