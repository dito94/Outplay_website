Overview

For those who want it all, the twin-motor Infinity series goes further. The louvres rotate, and they also retract completely to one end of the structure.

One press of a button and the roof disappears, opening the view to a clear sky by day or the stars at night. Infinity turns a sheltered terrace into a fully open space in seconds.

A roof that disappears in seconds

Aluminium pergolas with a motorised louvred roof make a terrace, garden or commercial space comfortable in every season. Built from high-grade aluminium, they give you full control of the climate beneath.

The louvres rotate smoothly from 0° to 120° and can gather at one end of the structure. Each louvre carries extra thermal and acoustic insulation, and when closed the roof keeps out sun, rain and snow.

Technology in the details

OUTPLAY offers two main series: the single-motor Balance for precise louvre rotation, and the twin-motor Infinity, whose roof elements both rotate and fully retract.

The structure can stand alone or attach to the building and is made in any RAL colour. Built-in perimeter gutters and posts that double as concealed downpipes carry rainwater away, and dimmable RGB lighting can be integrated into the profiles.

With vertical glazing, Infinity becomes a winter garden that can open completely to the sky on warm days.
