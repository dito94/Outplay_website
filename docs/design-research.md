### 7. skill-issue.in
The page itself was blocked; this comes from search.
- **What it is:** "A ranked library of agent skills, curated by what actually gets used." It lists SKILL.md files (instruction files for AI coding agents) by category (Design, Frontend, Backend, Research, Automation), ranked by GitHub stars and clicks.
- **Design leaders:** **taste-skill** (41.4k stars), ui-ux-pro-max-skill, frontend-design, design-system-patterns.
- **taste-skill's method** (README on GitHub):
  1. List what only this client has.
  2. Pick one of 20 researched art directions.
  3. Write one sentence that ties the client's own material to that direction.
  4. Use real photos and footage.
  5. Check the page in a real browser at three widths and fail it on generic AI patterns.
  6. Fix the top three problems, for up to three rounds.
- **Frame checks it runs:** a clear title on the first screen, equal margins, no section pushed to one side, no random blank stretches. Its motion is benchmarked on Awwwards Site of the Day winners.

### 8. impeccable.style: checklist
Read from the GitHub repo (SKILL.md v4.8.0, `craft-floor.md`, `typeset.md`, `animate.md`, `layout.md`, `colorize.md`) and the detector rule list in npm v3.6.1. The current version says it has 62 detector rules; I read about 50.

**Core stance**
- A marketing site is "Persuade" mode: bold, distinct, real imagery, full commitment.
- "The brief wins": the client's pinned colours and fonts override the warnings below.
- Pick light or dark from where and how the site is used, not from the industry.

**Typography**
- Avoid fonts it calls overused: **Inter, Roboto, Fraunces, Geist, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk**, Arial and system fonts. Don't use a system font as the display face.
- No oversized italic serif hero headline (Fraunces, Playfair, Recoleta).
- **No small uppercase label above a heading ("eyebrow" or "kicker"). This is the one outright ban.** No pill chip above the hero either.
- Size steps should differ by at least 1.25×. Display type at most 6rem. Letter-spacing no tighter than −0.04em.
- Long headlines should not be set at display size.
- Body text 16px minimum, line length 65–75 characters, line height 1.5–1.7. Clickable or label text never below 11px.
- No all-caps paragraphs. Letter-spacing on body text at most 0.05em. No justified text.
- Use `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `text-wrap: pretty` on body text.
- On dark backgrounds, add a little line height, letter-spacing and weight.
- Use tabular numbers for data. Load only the weights you use. Don't skip heading levels.

**Colour**
- Avoid purple/violet gradients, cyan on dark, and **cream/beige as the reflex "tasteful" background**.
- No gradient text. No coloured glows: zero-offset coloured shadows, radial halos, "spotlight" blobs.
- Never grey text on a coloured background; tint it from that colour instead.
- Prefer OKLCH for new palettes. Define colours by role: surfaces, text, action, borders, status.
- Let the strongest colour own one clear area or role. Don't spend the call-to-action colour on decoration.
- Contrast: 4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text, icons and focus rings.

**Layout**
- Avoid pages built from same-size cards (icon + heading + text). **Never nest cards.**
- No rounded-square icon tile above every heading. No 01/02/03 section numbers unless the order matters to the reader.
- No hero big-number template (big number, small label, supporting stats).
- Group by proximity before adding boxes. Spacing should alternate tight and generous, on a 4-point scale.
- More space above a heading than below it.
- Padding: at least 12–16px inside bordered or coloured boxes, at least 24–32px at the viewport edge.
- Use a modal only for tasks that need interruption.

**Surface details**
- No coloured left/right border stripe thicker than 1px on cards. No hairline border combined with a wide soft shadow.
- No hard offset shadows unless the design really is neobrutalist.
- No decorative glass or blur, grid-line backgrounds or striped gradients.
- No emoji or unicode as icons; use one consistent icon set.
- No big SVG "illustrations" assembled from basic shapes. No `clip-path` blobs standing in for real cut-outs.
- Photos must actually show, not sit buried under a near-opaque overlay.

**Motion**
- One authored focal moment, not the same fade-up on every section.
- Ease-out curve `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`. No bounce or elastic easing.
- Durations: 100–150 ms feedback, 150–300 ms state changes, 300–500 ms overlays, 500–800 ms focal entrance. Exits faster than entrances.
- Animate transform and opacity, plus blur, mask or clip-path where smooth. Never width, height or margins.
- Content must be visible by default; JavaScript only enhances the entrance.
- No auto-scrolling marquees, pulsing dots, fake blinking cursors, or **scale or rotate on image hover**.
- Provide a meaningful `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.

**Copy**
- No buzzwords (streamline, empower, world-class, cutting-edge, next-generation).
- No repeated punchy formulas like "Not X. Y." or "X. No Y."
- Don't overuse em-dashes. Buttons name their action. Error messages say what went wrong and how to fix it.

**Things most AI-built sites forget**
- Style text selection, caret, scrollbars, focus rings and underline offset from the palette.
- Design every state: hover, disabled, loading, error, empty.

## Design brief synthesis

This combines the confirmed product and information findings with the Impeccable and taste-skill principles. It does not draw on the client's visual taste, since I couldn't see the visual references.

1. **Pick one visual direction tied to the material:** architectural aluminium and glass in Sofia light. Write the one-sentence idea first, for example: "the terrace as a room you control with the light."
2. **Build the palette from what OUTPLAY sells:**
   - A tinted anthracite based on RAL 7016 (not pure black) and a cool aluminium-grey family.
   - One accent used only for calls to action, for example a deep garden green or a warm LED amber.
   - Define colours in OKLCH. Avoid a cream background, purple gradients and glows.
3. **Light pages for daytime, dark sections for evening.** Use dark only for sections that show night scenes with the LED lighting on.
4. **Fonts must have strong Cyrillic.** Body: Onest or Golos Text (both free, Cyrillic-first, not on the overused list). Display: a grotesk with Cyrillic support such as Suisse Int'l or TT Norms Pro, or Unbounded if the brand can carry it.
   - Set `lang="bg"` and check the font has the Bulgarian letterforms.
   - Test headlines against Bulgarian text, which runs longer than English.
5. **Hero:** full-bleed real photo or video of a built OUTPLAY project, a short headline, and one call to action ("Направете запитване"). No eyebrow label, chip, gradient text or stats row.
6. **One authored motion moment:** louvres rotating from 0° to 90° as the visitor scrolls, opening onto the sky. Elsewhere, motion only confirms actions or changes state, using the `0.16,1,0.3,1` curve. Provide a reduced-motion version.
7. **"What we do" graphic (the Artbey request):** a commissioned 3D render or photo of a house terrace with hotspots on each product family: roof, sliding, folding and guillotine glass, ZIP screens, awning.
   - Each hotspot opens a short panel linking to the product page. On mobile it becomes a stacked list.
   - Do not draw it as an SVG made of basic shapes; Impeccable explicitly flags that.
8. **Navigation:** a slim sticky bar with a products menu showing the five families with photos, a persistent quote button, a phone number and a BG/EN switch.
9. **Product page template** (based on Artbey and PergoHouse):
   1. Hero
   2. A row of key specs in tabular numbers: maximum span, wind (for example 117 km/h), snow load, RAL colours
   3. Variants (wall-mounted, freestanding, and so on)
   4. A gallery of built projects with locations
   5. Video
   6. "Combine with" ZIP screens or glass
   7. PDF catalogue
   8. FAQ
   9. Inline quote form
10. **Quote form, inline on the page rather than a modal, in three steps:**
    1. Mounting type, then approximate **width × depth (× height)** as linked sliders and number fields, with a live top-down diagram labelled ширина and дълбочина.
    2. Options: motor, sensors, LED, side closures.
    3. Name, phone, email, city, optional photo upload, GDPR consent.

    Show the next step clearly: оглед, then визуализация, then оферта (site visit, visualisation, offer), which is PergoHouse's process.
11. **Before/after slider** on project pages and one on the homepage: same camera angle, a draggable handle that also works with the keyboard (a range input), and short labels.
12. **FAQ accordion** with about ten questions modelled on Mirador's, adapted to Bulgaria: permit, timeline from visit to installation, foundation, waterproofing and drainage, wind and snow, adding glass or ZIP later, maintenance, warranty, colours, motor and sensors, free site visit, financing. Mark it up as schema.org FAQPage.
13. **Layout:** an editorial, photo-led, asymmetric layout. No grid of identical icon cards and no nested cards. Spacing alternates tight and generous on a 4-point scale; text measures 65–75 characters.
14. **Copy:** specific Bulgarian facts (materials, figures, Sofia projects) and no buzzwords. Trust comes from real projects, warranty, the process and financing, not invented statistics.
15. **Finish:** style selection, focus rings and scrollbars; tabular numbers in specs; no image zoom on hover; no logo marquee; AVIF images and video posters for fast loading; check at three widths before shipping.
